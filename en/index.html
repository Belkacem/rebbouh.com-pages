Projects

An AI agent platform

Personal project

I built a platform for assigning tasks to specialised agents, following their work and intervening when they need a decision.

The project integrates existing harnesses: the environments connecting a model to its instructions and tools. My work covers their integration, task orchestration, the user interface and infrastructure.

A few implementation details

Distributing work
Agents have distinct roles. Tasks can be delegated or triggered by scheduled routines or events.
Resuming a task
A retry checks the current state and uses an idempotency key to avoid duplicate executions. It cannot bypass a pause imposed by the budget.
Requesting a decision
Questions and approvals are attached to the task, with their context. Responses are attributed to their author and can wake the agent. Permissions are checked on the server.
Enforcing a budget
Cost tracking is connected to execution: a warning before the limit, a pause when it is reached, and resumption when the budget allows it.
Creating environments
Provisioning uses idempotent steps: after an interruption, existing resources are reused. Each organisation has its own database, secrets and container.
